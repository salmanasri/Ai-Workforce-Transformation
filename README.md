# Ai-Workforce-Transformation

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on the workforce from 2018 to 2035. The analysis focuses on AI adoption, employment changes, salaries, productivity, employee training, reskilling, workforce resilience, and AI transformation success.

The project uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to identify trends, relationships, and patterns in AI-driven workforce transformation.

## Aim

To analyze how AI adoption and automation are influencing employment, salaries, productivity, reskilling, and workforce resilience across different industries, regions, and occupations.

## Objectives

Analyze the trend of AI adoption over time.

Examine the impact of AI on employment and job changes.

Compare salary levels and salary growth across industries.

Analyze the relationship between AI adoption and productivity.

Study employee AI training and reskilling patterns.

Evaluate workforce resilience in response to AI transformation.

Identify industries and occupations with higher AI adoption.

Analyze factors associated with successful AI transformation.

Provide data-driven recommendations for organizations and workforce planning.

## Dataset

The dataset used in this project is the AI Workforce Transformation (2018–2035) dataset from Kaggle.

The dataset contains information related to:

AI adoption

Generative AI adoption

Automation

Employee count

Jobs created and displaced

Net employment change

Salaries

Productivity

AI training

Reskilling

Workforce resilience

AI transformation success

Industry

Occupation

Country and region
